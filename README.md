## 🚀 The Problem with Job Hunting Today

Applying for jobs is exhausting. Every company has a different job description, looking for specific keywords, unique phrasings, and highlighted experiences. 

If you use a **single, static resume** for every application, your chances of passing through the ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) and catching a recruiter's eye plummet. But manually editing your resume for *every single application* takes hours. 

You find yourself deleting valuable experience to fit a page limit, or digging through old documents to remember what you did at a job 5 years ago because it suddenly became relevant again.

**There has to be a better way.**


## 💡 The Solution: Resumio

**Resumio** flips the traditional resume paradigm on its head. Instead of maintaining an artificially constrained one-page PDF, you maintain a **Master Resume**. 

Your Master Resume is the single source of truth for your entire professional life. It contains every bullet point, every project, every skill, and every educational achievement you've ever earned. 

When you find a job you want to apply for, you simply **paste the Job Description into Resumio**. Our integrated AI (powered by Google Gemini) instantly cross-references the job requirements with your Master Resume, extracting *only* the most relevant experiences and highlighting the exact skills the employer is looking for.

You get a perfectly tailored, ATS-optimized, high-impact resume in seconds. 

## 🎯 Who is this for?

Resumio is designed to give you an unfair advantage in the job market. It is incredibly beneficial for:

- **The Serial Applicant:** Applying to 50+ jobs a month? Stop sending the same generic resume. Resumio allows you to submit 50 highly-targeted, custom resumes in the time it used to take you to write one cover letter.
- **The Career Pivot-er:** Trying to transition from Marketing to Product Management? Resumio's AI will highlight the specific cross-functional projects in your Master Resume that prove you have the exact skills the new role demands.
- **The Senior Professional:** If you have 10+ years of experience, a 1-page limit is agonizing. Store all 10 years in your Master Resume, and let Resumio extract only the 1-page worth of experience that matters for the specific role you are eyeing today.
- **The Freelancer / Contractor:** Juggling dozens of clients and diverse projects? Organize them all in the Master Resume, and generate targeted portfolios of your work tailored to whatever your next client is looking for.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🧠 The Master Repository:** A clean, intuitive dashboard to log every piece of your professional history. Never lose track of a great bullet point again.
- **⚡ One-Click Tailoring:** Paste a job description, click generate, and watch as AI drafts a custom resume that speaks directly to the hiring manager.
- **🔒 Secure & Private:** Built with Clerk Authentication and Supabase (PostgreSQL), ensuring your personal data is locked down and accessible only by you.
- **🎨 Beautiful Exports:** Export your tailored resumes to pixel-perfect, recruiter-approved PDF formats.
